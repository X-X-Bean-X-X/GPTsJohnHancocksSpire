A standalone rooftop broadcast mast inspired by the John Hancock Center's antennas in Chicago. The tapered white shaft has black clamps, clipped metal conduit, bent cable feeds and three junction boxes. The black mounting base has exposed anchor bolts. A ribbed red beacon and gold cap finish the tip.

### Placement

- Look under **Industrial Props** in the Editor or search for **GPT's John Hancock's Spire by X_Bean_X** in your prop browser.
- The mast is **60 m tall** with a **3 × 3 m base**. Its origin is the bottom center of the base, so line that point up with the roof surface.
- The red lens pulses about every **two seconds** at normal simulation speed. The animation is part of the asset.

The package has no required mods or DLC and includes three mesh detail levels. Tested on CS2 **1.6.4f1** through a fresh startup, ground and elevated placements, and repeated beacon cycles in the Editor.
